# EU Trade Dependency Analysis (2015-2024)

## Overview
Analysis of trade dependencies and economic resilience across four major EU economies: France, Germany, Italy, and Spain. This study examines supply chain concentration risks and Brexit's impact on trade patterns using UN Comtrade data.

## Research Questions
- Which EU countries rely too heavily on single partners for critical products?
- Are supply chains becoming more or less concentrated?
- How did EU-UK trade change after Brexit?
- Did EU countries successfully replace UK imports with other partners?

## Key Findings
- **Critical Dependencies Identified:**
  - China dominates textiles (79-83%) and electronics (69-80%) across all four countries
  - USA controls pharmaceutical imports for Spain (79%), Italy (69%), and Germany (69%)
  - Italy has the highest vulnerabilities with 6 high-risk dependencies
  
- **Brexit Impact:**
  - EU-UK trade dropped 18% (exports) and 14% (imports) in 2020
  - Fruits imports from UK collapsed by 88%
  - EU countries shifted to Nigeria, India, and Egypt for electronics and fruits

## Methodology
- **Data Source:** UN Comtrade Database (2015-2024)
- **Countries Analyzed:** France, Germany, Italy, Spain
- **Trading Partners:** USA, China, Russia, UK, Japan, India, Taiwan, Nigeria, South Africa, Egypt
- **Product Categories:** Fruits, Cereals, Minerals, Fuels, Pharmaceuticals, Plastics, Textiles, Machinery, Electronics, Vehicles
- **Tools:** R (data analysis and visualization)
